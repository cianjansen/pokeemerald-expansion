CMroute1 - Next Trainers (planning notes)
==========================================

Roster:
- Grandma 1: posh
- Grandma 2: posh
- Fisherman 1: retired, chill guy
- Fisherman 2: rich boat guy
- Biker 1: young biker
- Biker 2: older biker

Available Pokemon pool: Scyther, Kricketot, Bagon, Buizel, Bidoof, Roselia,
Machop, Totodile, Treecko, Taillow

Levels: roughly 12-18. Each trainer has 2 or 3 Pokemon.

Biker 1 - young biker - 2 Pokemon

Biker 2 - older biker - 3 Pokemon
